## Where it stands

Five progressive webcam demos with MediaPipe (hand tracking, face mesh, both together, interaction basics, a face instrument), a gallery, a "Beyond Face and Hands" overview of five more MediaPipe tasks, and guides for working in OpenProcessing or a local server. The last work (9 to 11 April 2026) moved all five demos to the MediaPipe Tasks Vision API, added status banners and camera handling, and added a Vite dev server with an HTTPS tunnel for phones. Nothing has changed since.
